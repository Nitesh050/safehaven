Hand-made, committed inputs (small files only). See IMPLEMENTATION.md, Day 1.

- `zone.geojson` – zone polygon (EPSG:4326)
- `breach.geojson` – breach point (with `start`/`end` ISO times) and embankment line
- `official_sites.csv` – name, lat, lon, type, capacity, source
- `river_daily.csv` – date, station, level_m, warning_m, danger_m, birpur_cumecs, source
- `validation.csv` – village, lat, lon, flooded_on, source
- `LICENSES.md` – source + licence of every dataset, photo and clip
